Final Diagnosis
Rothia mucilaginosa , formerly known as Stomatococcus mucilaginosus , was identified.
The gram stain was derived from a blood culture, aerobic bottle, obtained from a patient with endocarditis. It reveals large round clusters of gram positive organisms, which were noted to be sticky and adherent to the plate.
