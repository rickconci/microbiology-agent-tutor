Final Diagnosis
Vasculitic lesions of histoplasmosis ( Figure 1 ) in an African patient with human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection and acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (AIDS).
The patient was treated with itraconazole at 5 mg/kg, and the lesions cleared. (See Figure 2 .)
