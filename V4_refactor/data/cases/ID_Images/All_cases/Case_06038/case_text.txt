Final Diagnosis
Synergistic necrotizing cellulitis. A gram stain of a swab of the "dishwater" exudate is shown in Figure 2 and reveals mixed gram negative and gram positive bacteria. In a patient with a peripheral leucocytosis, there is a notable absence of polymorphonuclear leukocytes on the gram stain, which is characteristic of this condition.
