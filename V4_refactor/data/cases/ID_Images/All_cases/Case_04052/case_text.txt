Final Diagnosis
Echinococcus granulosus infection of the pelvis and liver.
The radiographs revealed extensive lytic lesions in the right pubic ramus with a pathological fracture, sequestrum and a calcified cyst ( Figures 5 through 7 with arrows). Additional radiographic evaluation revealed a concomitant three-centimeter heavily calcified liver cyst ( Figure 8 with arrows). An immunoblot assay performed by the Center for Disease Control was positive for Echinococcus granulosus .
